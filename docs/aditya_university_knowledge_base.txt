OVERVIEW AND HISTORY
--------------------
Aditya University is a private state university located in Surampalem, Kakinada District, Andhra Pradesh, India.
The university was officially established in 2023 under the Andhra Pradesh Private Universities Act, 2016.
It has evolved from the legacy of Aditya Engineering College (AEC), which was founded in 2001 under the Aditya Academy educational group.
The university is a multidisciplinary institution offering undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs.

ADMISSION PROCESS
-----------------
Admission to Aditya University is based on merit and entrance exam scores.

Accepted Entrance Exams:
- ASAT (Aditya Scholastic Aptitude Test) - conducted by the university itself
- AP EAPCET (for Engineering and Pharmacy)
- JEE Main (for B.Tech admissions)
- CAT, CMAT, MAT, XAT (for MBA admissions)
- State or national level exams based on the program

Application Mode: Online and Offline through the official university portal (www.adityauniversity.in)

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
--------------------

For Undergraduate (UG) Programs:
- Candidates must have completed 10+2 (Intermediate) or equivalent from a recognized board.
- Minimum 60% marks required for B.Tech and Pharmacy programs.
- Minimum 55% marks required for BBA, BCA, and B.Sc programs.

For Postgraduate (PG) Programs:
- Candidates must hold a UG degree or equivalent in a relevant discipline.
- Minimum 60% marks in the qualifying degree is mandatory.

For Ph.D Programs:
- Candidates must have completed a Postgraduate degree in the relevant field.
- Minimum 55% for Math, Physics, Chemistry, English, Management, and Computer Applications.
- Minimum 60% for all Engineering disciplines.

HOSTEL RULES AND REGULATIONS
------------------------------

General Conduct:
- Students are expected to maintain discipline, cleanliness, and decorum at all times.
- Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited and is a punishable offense under law.
- Residents are responsible for the upkeep and cleanliness of their rooms.

Outing Rules:
- Hostel students must attend mandatory evening roll calls.
- Students are required to be inside the hostel by the designated curfew time.
- Leaving the campus or staying overnight outside requires prior written permission from the warden and parent/guardian approval.
- Day scholars visiting hostels are not permitted without prior approval.

Room Policies:
- Changing rooms or interchanging furniture without authorization from the warden is strictly forbidden.
- Students must not damage hostel property; damage will be recovered from the student.
- Visitors of the opposite gender are not allowed inside hostel rooms.

Prohibited Items and Activities:
- Possession or consumption of alcohol, tobacco, drugs, or any intoxicants is strictly prohibited.
- Violation leads to immediate disciplinary action, including potential expulsion from the hostel and the university.
- Use of electric heaters, irons, or personal cooking appliances inside rooms is not permitted.
- Loud music or noise that disturbs other residents is prohibited, especially during study hours.

STUDENT REGULATIONS AND CODE OF CONDUCT
-----------------------------------------

Academic Regulations:
- A minimum attendance of 75% is mandatory to be eligible for examinations.
- Students with less than 75% attendance may be detained and barred from appearing in exams.
- Academic malpractice during examinations is strictly penalized.
- Disturbing others during designated study hours is not allowed.

Campus Discipline:

- Dress code as prescribed by the university must be followed.

ABOUT ADITYA EDUCATIONAL GROUP
--------------------------------
The Aditya Educational Group has been a prominent educational institution in Andhra Pradesh since 2001.
The group started with Aditya Engineering College (AEC) in Surampalem and has grown into a full university.
The institution is known for academic excellence, strong industry connections, and holistic student development.
The group also includes other institutions such as Aditya College of Engineering and Technology (ACET) and other affiliated colleges.
The transition to a full university in 2023 marked a new era of expanded programs and research opportunities.
